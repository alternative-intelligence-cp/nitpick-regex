## 1. What was measured, and what was designed — the facts this plan rests on

### 1.1 The starting tree

0.1.0b's step 7: `250/250` at `5fbaf4a`, its five decisions recorded, `AstNode` and `AstKind` deriving `Copy`, a
runner that counts rejection sites and holds the target pins. `parse.npk` holds `parse_check_length` and nothing
else; `PatternErrorKind` has thirty-seven kinds.

### 1.2 The parser, as `step2.patch` writes it (`SYNTAX.md` Y-33)

- **The entry.** `parse_pattern(uint8[]:pat, Ast->:out) -> PatternError?` — RX-175's shape. It refuses the length
  (`parse_check_length`, 0.1.0's), then the encoding (`parse_check_encoding`, new), then builds a `Parser` and
  walks. The `Parser` holds the cursor, the frame being built, the next capture index and two `Vec`s — the saved
  frames and the named groups — so it is move-only; `parse_pattern` frees both on every path (a leaked `wild`
  block traps at exit).
- **The walk.** One `while (!cursor_at_end) decreases pat.len - pos`, one byte read per turn and dispatched: `|`
  closes the current alternative; `(` opens a group (`open_group`); `)` closes one (`close_group`); `*` `+` `?`
  `{` quantify (`quantify`); `.` `^` `$` are atoms; `\` is an escape (`escape`); `[` is refused provisionally;
  anything else is a literal, its UTF-8 continuation bytes consumed with it. Every helper that reads further
  consumes at least what it reads, so the measure falls every turn.
- **A frame** (`Frame`, thirteen fields, `#[derive(Copy)]`) holds two lists — the alternatives closed so far,
  and the current alternative's pieces — each a first node, a last node and a count, linked by rewriting the node
  before; and the last atom PENDING, not yet linked. The next atom, `|`, `)` or the end links it; a quantifier
  wraps it in a `Repeat` first. `(` links the pending atom, saves the frame on the stack, and starts the group's;
  `)` closes the body, pushes the `Group` node, pops the saved frame, and makes the group its pending atom.
- **Closing.** An alternative of no piece is an `Empty` at the offset where it ends; of one, that piece; of more,
  a `Concat`. A body of one alternative is that alternative; of more, an `Alternate`. At the end, an open group is
  `UnclosedGroup` at the innermost `(`; otherwise the body becomes the root.
- **No function calls itself.** Cycle 0.1.2 adds the bound (`NREGEX_NEST_DEPTH`, at `push_group`) and the tree
  check that says so.

### 1.3 Measured with the parser in place, at `5fbaf4a`

- **Every shape parses as designed**: `tests/unit/parse_grammar.npk`, fifty-nine cases — each pattern's tree
  dumped as text (with spans for nine of them) and compared whole; the dump also checks list counts, `next` links
  and flags — `0` at −O0 and through `opt -O2`. A wrong expectation on one case exits with its number (block 2).
- **Every refusal is exact**: `parse_refusals.npk`, sixty-three cases of kind, offset, span and detail;
  `parse_encoding.npk`, seventeen ill-formed sequences and nine boundaries decoded; `parse_limits.npk`, the capture
  bound at 250 and 251 (named groups too), the length at 65 536 and 65 537 through `parse_pattern`, and 250 groups
  nested — the stack holds them without a native frame each.
- **The bill does not move**: a program importing `syntax.npk` owes `core`'s eleven arms (block 2's `bills`), and
  `parse_grammar`'s object references only the floor and B-2's reviewed residue (`undefs`) — every tree check
  green, including `check_constants_named` over the parser's byte constants (each named, never a bare literal in a
  comparison) and `check_vec_elements_own_nothing` over `Vec<Frame>` and `Vec<GroupName>`.
- **The text costs nothing new**: `pattern_error_text` builds in a `Bytes` with `bytes_put_uint`, so no division
  (S-25) and no symbol beyond `npk_string_concat` and `npk_string_from_bytes`, both reviewed. A template with
  `&{…}` was measured and declined: it references `npk_int_to_string`, which is not.
- **Four language facts met while writing it**, each now a line in §4: `end` and `unit` are keywords (a
  parameter named either is `PARSE-002`); `string_bytes(f(x))` is a view of a temporary (`BORROW-012`: bind it);
  a pointer cannot become an integer (`TYPE-032`: a test cannot print through `sys`); and `else if` chains parse.

### 1.4 What §1 left open, decided (§2 has the alternatives)

- **A literal is every character but twelve** metacharacters, `\ . ^ $ | ? * + ( ) [ {` — a bare `]` or `}` is a
  literal (Y-28).
- **The whole pattern is checked as UTF-8 first**, against RFC 3629's table, before any grammar (Y-28, per
  [`../../research/CURRENCY.md`](../../research/CURRENCY.md)) — so a later subcycle decodes without checking.
- **`{` always begins a bound**; an unfinished one is `BadRepeatBounds`, never a literal (Y-32).
- **`UnclosedGroup` names the innermost open `(`**, its detail how many are open (Y-29).
- **§8's group-head refusals are made at the head** — lookaround, the atomic group, recursion, `(?P=`, the comment
  group — and a `+` after a quantifier is `AtomicGroupUnsupported` (Y-30). §8's escapes stay 0.1.5's.
- **What 0.1.1 cannot parse yet is refused provisionally** — `[`, an escape other than punctuation, a flag — with
  the kind their parser gives an unknown member, and no test pins it (Y-31).
- **`EmptyAlternate` is retired** (§9's list, thirty-six kinds).

### 1.5 The error surface (the author's bar: what, where, the likely fix)

Every refusal carries Y-10's offset and span and a `detail` chosen for its message — the quantifier's byte, the
bound, the first group's number, the offending character, whether a name was empty or unfinished (`SYNTAX.md`
§9's new table). `pattern_error_text` renders one sentence per kind, e.g. for `(a(b`: *"unclosed group at byte 2:
this `(` has no matching `)`; 2 groups are still open at the end of the pattern, and this is the innermost. Add a
`)`, or write `\(` to match a parenthesis."* — and for `(?P<n>a)`: *"wrong named-group spelling at byte 0:
`(?P<name>...)` is Python's spelling. Write `(?<name>...)`."* Twenty-two of 0.1.1's sentences are held to the
letter by `tests/unit/pattern_error_text.npk`, and every kind renders a sentence naming its byte. `API.md` put the
function at 0.10.5; it is written here, beside the kinds, and 0.10.5 only re-exports it (Y-34).

### 1.6 What 0.1.0 handed on, answered

`parse_pattern` has 0.1.0's signature, calls `parse_check_length` first, and is re-exported on one line; the node
table is Y-27's unchanged; which unclosed `(` an error names is decided (the innermost); flags are stamped on
every node, `AST_FLAG_U`; `InvalidPatternEncoding` is judged — for the whole pattern, first; siblings are linked
by rewriting the node before, and the pending atom is what a frame keeps. No lookahead beyond one byte was needed:
every group head and quantifier consumes as it reads.

## 4. What will bite

- **`parse.npk` is new code in a language with sharp edges**, and the patch is the tested text. A worker who
  tightens it re-runs every block from step 2, and a changed position or message is a changed Expect only if the
  record says why.
- **Reserved words refused as binding names**: `end` (use `upto`), `unit`, `in`, `range`, `any`, `stack`,
  `error`, `move` … — a parameter so named is `PARSE-002`, sometimes far from the name.
- **`string_bytes(f(x))` is `BORROW-012`**: bind the string first, then take its view.
- **A comparison against a bare literal outside `limits.npk` fails `check_constants_named`** unless the value is
  in its small set: every byte value the parser compares with `<`, `>`, `<=` or `>=` is a named `fixed` (`CH_…`,
  `U8_…`); `==` against a literal is fine.
- **A `Vec` element must own nothing** (`check_vec_elements_own_nothing`), and `vec_get` needs `T: Copy`: `Frame`
  and `GroupName` derive it and hold only integers.
- **Every exit path of `parse_pattern` frees both `Vec`s** — a leaked `wild` block traps at exit (D-151). The walk
  answers through one call so the frees run once, after it.
- **A unit exits with a case number below 91** — 91 … 96, 99 and 106 … 117 are `failsafe` arms, and an exit
  status is one byte.
- **The provisional refusals are not pinned** — no test asserts `[`, `\n` or `(?i)`'s kind; 0.1.3 and 0.1.4 own
  them (Y-31).
- **`#[derive(Copy)]` needs every member `Copy`** — a new enum field in `Frame` needs its enum to derive it.
- **`grep` is ugrep**, earlyoom is live, and every `rm` names its target through `${VAR:?}` — as 0.1.0b's §4.
