### 1.2 RX-032's reason is false since `c3bdae2`, and the rule is not

RX-032 and S-18 say a recursive-descent parser on `((((…` *"blows the call stack, and the language has no stack-depth
guard — the failure is a segfault"*. Since the compiler's `c3bdae2` every emitted function carries LLVM's split-stack
prologue, which compares the stack against its thread's limit before the frame exists — a leaf with no frame alone
skips it — and `__morestack` traps `StackExhausted`, `failsafe` running on a stack of its own (D-305). Measured with
probe 19, a recursive descent over nested `(` with a frame the size of `parse.npk`'s `Frame` (block 1):

- 2^20 levels leave through `failsafe`'s `StackExhausted` arm, exit 106, at −O0 and through `opt -O2` — never on a
  signal;
- −O0 runs 16 911 levels and traps at 16 912; `opt -O2` runs 52 425 and traps at 52 426 — each edge the same on
  twenty runs of each side, the main thread's stack being the floor's own 8 MiB mapping (D-305 (3));
- so **10 000 levels trap at neither leg**, and **65 536** — `NREGEX_PATTERN_BYTES` of `(` — traps at both.

The rule stands for what is still true: a trap stops the consumer's whole program, where S-9 owes it a
`PatternError`; and the depth a recursion survives is the optimiser's frame and the caller's thread (a spawned
thread has 2 MiB), threefold apart between two legs of one program. PD-35.

### 1.3 The parser today: an explicit stack with no bound

`unbounded.npk.txt`, run against `679ea43`'s parser (block 0b), exits 0 — every answer below holds:

- `(` 251 times, `(` 10 000 times with 10 000 closers, and `(` 65 536 times are each `TooManyCaptureGroups` at byte
  250, detail 250: the 251st `(` is past the capture bound too, and `take_cap` answers first. The deepest, most
  obvious attack pattern is refused for a reason that is not its own.
- `(?:` 21 845 times and `a` — 65 536 bytes — is `UnclosedGroup` at byte 65 532 with 21 845 groups open: the walk read

So nothing crashes and nothing is bounded: the explicit stack RX-182 built works, and 0.1.2's work is the refusal.

### 1.4 Where the bound is decided: at the `(`, before the head

0.1.1's §8 put the check in `push_group`. `push_group` runs after the head is read and after `take_cap`, so there
`((((…` would still answer `TooManyCaptureGroups` (§1.3) and a named group's name would be judged first. Decided at
the top of `open_group` instead — the `(` read while 250 groups are open — the answer is `NestTooDeep` at that `(`
whatever follows it, nothing past it is read, and no frame past the bound is pushed (probe 05's property, in the
parser). That pre-empts, for such a `(` only, RX-185's *"the 251st is `TooManyCaptureGroups` at its `(`"*, its §8

at byte 250, and a lookahead or a closing `(?` behind the 251st `(` at its byte, each with no node built
