## 2. The decisions, and what chose each

Three, **accepted at this plan's verifier's PASS** and recorded as the next three RX numbers, in PD order, under one
batch heading for the subcycle, *"Normalisation — cycle 0.2.3"* — the heading, the first and the second by step 1's

hunks. §1 is their evidence.

| Label | Decision | Why this and not the alternative |
|---|---|---|
| **PD-120** | **What *"structurally equal"* is.** Two patterns are structurally equal when the trees `hir_build` builds for them are equal up to H-13's rewrites, each read as an identity, and the two H-16's `Concat` row applies — a `Concat` of no piece is `Empty`, of one that piece. So a `Concat`'s `Empty` piece goes — `Empty` is concatenation's identity; an alternation is flattened in order and an `Empty` alternative stays; a class is the set its ranges cover (Y-15), sorted and merged, and of one codepoint that `Literal`, its byte bit kept; a literal run is the adjacent `Literal` pieces of one flat `Concat`, no node of its own; and nothing else joins — an alternative's place, a group, a repetition's bounds and laziness, an anchor's or a boundary's kind, and the byte bit: `(literal byte 97)` and `(literal 97)` are two HIRs. H-13 and Y-15 dated | **H-13's rewrites are the only text that says what is one structure** (finding 1), and read as identities they decide the two cases the rule leaves open (findings 2, 3). ## 3. The steps, and what each commit carries

**The definition and its normaliser, then the Gate, then what a run leaves, then the prose, then the record.** Steps
1 … 4 each apply one patch, which `apply.py` STAGES, and each is committed at its last block's end — exactly the staged
files, with the dispatch's `ATTRIBUTION` lines and the subject the step names. Step 0 changes nothing. This file's title
is set `RUNNING (since <date>, <stream>)` after block 0h, uncommitted, and lands with the record. Every commit:
`check_refs` clean (each step's block runs it once its patch is staged); `git status --short` read. **Nothing is pushed
until step 6**: GitHub runs a push's head only, so the four work commits are read in CI through the head's run.

**The order is load-bearing.** The normaliser and its units before the Gate that leans on both; the mutants over both;
the prose last, since it states the counts. Step 3 changes no program the harness builds, so its full run is step 4's.
