## Description (349 char limit; this is 197)

Regular expressions for Nitpick — linear time guaranteed on every pattern and every input. Automata-based, zero dependency, no libc, no syscalls. Catastrophic backtracking is not slow here; it is impossible.

## Notes for the applier

- "redos" and "redos-protection" are the two that will actually be searched by
  someone with the problem this library solves.
- "pike-vm" and "thompson-nfa" are low-traffic but precise: they find the
  audience that already knows why backtracking is the wrong answer.
- Deliberately NOT included: "pcre" (we are not PCRE-compatible and the topic
  would mislead), "backtracking" (we are the opposite), "no-libc" (true, but
  "zero-dependency" already says it and 20 is the cap).
